Source: Michael's list of target vacancies (Desktop/amsterdam-java-vacancies.html, 62 employers, 30
open roles analysed; 13 full postings read: Adyen, Picnic, OpenValue, Axxes, JDriven, Team

consultancies/secondment (~24), plus product companies (Adyen, Picnic, Prodin) and government

communication; pragmatism; independence; mentoring/code review; Scrum/Agile/DevOps teams.
